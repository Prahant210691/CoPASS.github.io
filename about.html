We Are Industry Expert

The desire of Founder Director Anil Jadhav to stay ahead of the latest trends led to the founding of CoPASS Technologies. Launched in 1996 as a software solution company aimed at creating customized solutions for cooperative credit societies, it has continuously evolved to keep pace with the dynamic changes in the Finance sector.

CoPASS now specializes in AI-powered digital solutions for Financial Institutions. Our expertise in development, customization, design and innovation enables us to provide comprehensive, user-friendly digital solutions that streamline end to end operations. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and optimizing processes, we ensure that our clients can operate more profitably and efficiently.
